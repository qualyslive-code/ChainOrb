ChainOrb Privacy Policy

Effective: October 19, 2026

ChainOrb is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it as small as we can: there are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

1. Information we collect

  • Random player ID. The first time you play, the game creates a random ID on your device. It is not linked to your name, email or phone, and it lets us keep one score per player and avoid duplicates.
  • Display name. The game gives you an automatic name such as "Orb1234". It is shown on leaderboards.
  • Country code. Taken from your device's language and region setting (for example "UG"), not from GPS. It is used for country flags and rankings.
  • Gameplay data. The level you played, the result, score, biggest chain, pieces placed, time taken, number of attempts and the game version. For the Daily Chain we also receive your move log so our server can check your score.

Your progress, settings and unlocked levels are saved on your device. The game also keeps its latest error message on your device only; it is not sent to us.

2. How we use it

  • To run the Daily Chain and leaderboards and to check that scores are fair.
  • To understand which levels are too hard or too easy, and to fix problems.
  • To prevent cheating and abuse.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it to build advertising profiles.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

ChainOrb shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ads software may collect your IP address, your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information, and uses them to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. Details are in Google's advertising policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device's Ads settings (usually under Settings → Privacy or Google → Ads).

4. Who holds your data

Gameplay and leaderboard data is stored with our service provider Supabase, in a data center in Ireland (EU). Ad data is handled by Google. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We do not share your data with anyone else except where the law requires it.

5. What others can see

Other players can see your display name, country and score on the leaderboards. Nothing else is shown.

6. How long we keep data

We keep gameplay records for as long as they are needed to run the leaderboards and improve the game, and we delete them on request (see below). Uninstalling the app removes the data saved on your device.

7. Your choices and deleting your data

You can ask us to delete the data we hold for you. Open Settings in the game, copy your Player ID, and email it to the address below. We will delete the records tied to that ID. Note that deleting the app does not by itself delete server records.

If you are in the EU/UK or another region with data-protection rights, you may also ask for access to or correction of your data, or object to its use, using the same address.

8. Children

ChainOrb is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

If we change how the game handles data, we will update this page and its date. Material changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.
